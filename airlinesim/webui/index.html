About the network map

An aircraft is drawn for every route that actually operated this tick, and it moves at its type's cruise speed — but its position along the leg is DERIVED from the clock, not simulated: the engine models daily frequency, not individual flights. Route endpoints, carriers, frequencies, airport conditions and weather systems are read straight from the model. Base map: Natural Earth (public domain) — coast, state lines, lakes, rivers and Interstates. It is geography, not shaded relief; there is no elevation data in this repo. The window is the lower 48.
Click an aircraft or a route to highlight it in the Fleet and Routes panels; click empty map to clear.
